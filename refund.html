Refund Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

We want you to be happy with Moments. This policy covers Premium ($19/month) and Pro Unlimited ($29/month). The Free Starter plan costs nothing.

1. 14-day refund

If you're not satisfied with your first paid subscription, request a refund within 14 days of your initial purchase and we will refund it in full, no questions asked.

2. Renewals

Subscriptions renew monthly. Renewal payments are generally not refundable, but if you were charged by mistake or forgot to cancel, contact us within 7 days of the charge and we will review it in good faith.

3. Cancelling

You can cancel anytime. You keep paid features until the end of the current billing period, and you won't be charged again. Cancelling does not by itself trigger a refund.

Email YOUR_EMAIL@ciphree.com.et from your account email with your order or receipt number. Our payment partner, Paddle, processes refunds, and they go back to your original payment method. Timing depends on your bank, usually 5 to 10 business days.

We may decline refunds where there is evidence of abuse or a breach of our Terms of Service. This does not limit any rights you have under applicable consumer law.
