Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Premium Chatbox Matchmaker pair users? ↓

Rather than browsing static profile cards, you type a specific topic, thought, or story into the chatbox and the matchmaker instantly pairs you with another active user seeking that exact conversation.

Why are public likes and view counters completely removed? ↓

Social algorithms treat human emotions as engagement bait. By hiding public likes, view counts, and follower totals, users write honest life experiences instead of chasing viral dopamine loops.

Can I connect with people anonymously using a pseudonym? ↓

Yes. You can publish shared stories or enter chat matchmaking under a persistent pen name. You control how much of your identity you reveal.

Are payments billed internationally in USD ($)? ↓

Yes. Premium ($19 USD/mo) and Pro ($29 USD/mo) memberships are processed securely worldwide in USD ($) with no hidden conversion fees.

What happens if someone sends offensive messages in chat matchmaking? ↓

Connections are strictly 1-on-1 and opt-in. Either user can leave, or contact us for further help.

Do I need to download an app store app to use Moments? ↓

No. Moments is a Progressive Web Application (PWA) that installs directly to your home screen or desktop browser without needing the iOS App Store or Google Play Store.
