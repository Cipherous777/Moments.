Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This policy explains what Moments (operated by Ciphree) collects, why, and the choices you have. We don't sell your data and we don't run ads.

1. What we collect

  • Account data: email address, pen name, and login details.
  • Content: Moments, Stories, and chat messages you send.
  • Usage and device data: basic technical information (browser, device type, app events) needed to run and secure the service.
  • Subscription data: your plan and billing status. Payments are handled by Paddle; we never see or store your full card details.

2. How we use it

To run Moments, authenticate you, enable matchmaking and chat, apply plan limits, process subscriptions, prevent abuse, provide support, and improve the product. Matchmaking uses the topics and interests you submit to pair you with others.

3. Who processes it

  • Google Firebase for authentication, database, and hosting of app data.
  • Paddle, our Merchant of Record, for payments, taxes, and invoices. Paddle acts as an independent controller of your billing data under its privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare and GitHub Pages for DNS and site delivery.

We share data only with these providers as needed to operate the service, or when the law requires it. Your data may be processed in countries other than your own.

4. Pseudonyms and visibility

You can use a pen name. Your Moments and Stories are visible to other users as the service allows; chats are visible only to the participants. Don't post information you wouldn't want others to see.

5. Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account we remove or anonymise your personal data, except what we must keep for legal, tax, or security reasons.

6. Your rights

You can access, correct, export, or delete your data by contacting us. If privacy law in your region gives you further rights, we will honour them.

7. Cookies and local storage

We use only essential cookies and local storage for sign-in, preferences, and security. We don't use advertising trackers.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical measures, but no system is completely secure.

9. Children

Moments is for people 18 and over. If we learn that a younger person has an account, we will delete it.

10. Changes

We will post updates here with a new date.
